# Weekly Retrospective — Week of September 20, 2026

## One thing I learned this week

Bug replication tests are not just a quality practice — they are a proof of understanding. If a developer can write a failing test that describes the bug, they understood the problem. If they can't, they didn't. This applies especially when AI tools are involved in writing the fix.

## One decision I made and why

Decided to introduce team-wide rules on PR standards and AI usage rather than addressing the developer's specific behavior one-on-one. The reasoning: depersonalizing the standard protects the individual while setting a clear expectation for everyone. Rules apply to everyone, support is personalized.

## One thing I want to do differently next week

Have the private conversation with the developer in French before sending the team-wide Teams message — so he doesn't feel blindsided reading it alongside everyone else.
