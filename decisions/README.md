# Decisions

A log of important technical and leadership decisions, with context and outcomes.

## Why keep this?

When something breaks in production or a decision gets questioned, having a record of what you decided, why, and what you knew at the time is invaluable. It also helps you learn from patterns over time.

## Template

```markdown
## [Decision title] — [Date]

### Context
What situation led to this decision?

### Options considered
1. 
2. 

### Decision made
What did you choose and why?

### Outcome
What happened? (fill in later)
```
